# Customer-Churn-Analysis-Dashboard
Developed a Power BI Customer Churn Dashboard to analyze retention, contract types, payment methods, and customer behavior, identifying churn patterns to improve customer retention.
 Project Overview

The Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze customer churn, retention patterns, and customer behavior. It provides insights into customer status, contract types, payment methods, gender distribution, tenure, and total revenue to support data-driven business decisions.

 Objectives

Analyze customer churn and retention rates.

Compare customer status across different contract types.

Understand customer distribution by payment method and gender.

Analyze average customer tenure by status.

Explore the relationship between customer tenure and total charges.

Identify patterns that can help improve customer retention.

 Tools & Technologies

Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization

Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation

DAX – Measures and calculations

Data Visualization – Charts, KPIs, and interactive reports

 Dashboard Features

Customer Status Analysis: Visualizes stayed, churned, and joined customers.

Contract Analysis: Compares customer status across monthly, one-year, and two-year contracts.

Payment Method Analysis: Shows customer distribution by cash, credit card, and mailed check.

Gender Analysis: Compares customer distribution and revenue by gender.

Tenure Analysis: Examines average tenure across customer statuses.

Tenure vs. Total Charges: Visualizes how total charges vary with customer tenure.

 Key Insights

The dashboard summarizes approximately 7,043 customers.

Approximately 67% of customers stayed, while around 27% churned.

Monthly contract customers show a notable churn pattern compared with longer-term contract customers.

Customers who stayed have a higher average tenure than churned customers.

Total charges generally increase with customer tenure.

Business Benefits

Supports customer retention strategies.

Helps identify customer groups with higher churn.

Provides insights into contract preferences and customer behavior.

Supports data-driven decisions to improve customer satisfaction and retention.
